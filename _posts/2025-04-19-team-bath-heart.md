### 🧩 Problem Statement

Team Bath Heart is a multidisciplinary team focused on creating novel medical solutions for cardiovascular patients. This project aimed to develop a concept-stage device for assisting in the diagnosis and monitoring of heart-related conditions. The challenge was to design a system that was compact, accurate, and cost-effective while accounting for user ergonomics and long-term wearability.

---

### 🛠 Tools & Methods Used

- Early-stage **prototyping** and **component selection**  
- Collaboration with medical advisors and clinical stakeholders  
- CAD design for form factor exploration  
- Evaluation of **biometric sensors** and signal processing concepts  
- Requirements gathering for regulatory compliance and patient safety

---

### 🚧 Challenges & Solutions

- Balancing technical feasibility with medical usability  
- Selecting the right biometric sensors for reliable long-term measurement  
- Designing a modular concept that could evolve based on real user feedback  
- Communicating across disciplines (engineering, health sciences, and business)

---

### ✅ Results & Impact

- Delivered a concept-stage design and technical feasibility report  
- Participated in internal pitch events and design reviews  
- Reinforced understanding of **user-centered design** and **early-stage medtech validation**  
- Experience contributed to deeper interest in health and rehabilitation tech
